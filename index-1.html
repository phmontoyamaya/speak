    What this measures

    Why not accent

    Accentedness and comprehensibility are separable, and they correlate weakly: a strongly accented speaker can be perfectly easy to understand (Munro & Derwing 1995, 1999; Levis 2005). Listener judgements of a speaker's competence track pause placement, filler density, pace, and pitch movement far more than phoneme identity. Those respond well to short daily training. Nativeness, after decades of L2 use, mostly does not.

    Lev-Ari & Keysar (2010) did find accented speech rated as less credible, apparently via processing fluency. The remedy that follows is slower, better-marked speech — which is what this app drills.

    Articulation rate vs. speech rate

    Speech rate counts every second including silence. Articulation rate counts only the time you were actually producing words. The gap between them is your pause budget. Two speakers at the same speech rate can be completely different to listen to: one talks fast and pauses well, the other talks steadily and never breathes.

    Filler counting, honestly

    Chrome's speech recognizer silently discards "um" and "uh" — it is built to clean up transcripts, not to score them. So those are caught in the audio instead: a voiced stretch over ~260 ms that is flat in pitch and flat in energy is almost always a filled pause. Words like "like", "basically", and "you know" come from the transcript, where they survive.

    The acoustic half is a heuristic. It will occasionally flag a held vowel in ordinary speech, and it will miss a very short "uh". Treat the trend across sessions as the signal, not any single count.

    Silence is the replacement

    The point of the filler drill is not to say fewer words. It's to move the hesitation from voiced to silent. Watch the swap ratio: pauses per filler. If fillers fall and pause count rises, that's the habit changing. If both fall, you're just speaking less.

    Your baseline, not a norm

    The first five sessions calibrate. After that every metric is scored against your own median rather than a published threshold, because published thresholds for spontaneous speech vary enormously by task, register, and language background. Reference bands are drawn faintly for orientation only.

    What it can't see

    It cannot judge whether your argument was any good, whether the evidence supported the claim, or whether you answered the actual question. That's the part to export and hand to a person — or to Claude — for a read. The app handles the mechanics; the mechanics are just the part that's measurable.

    Data

    Sessions are stored in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded, and no audio is ever saved — only the numbers and the transcript. Clearing your browser data will erase your history, so export a backup every few weeks.
